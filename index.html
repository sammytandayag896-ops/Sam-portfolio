My Story

My interest in business started long before I had any formal experience. Growing up, I was always curious about how people built businesses, solved problems, and created opportunities. I often looked for ways to help, whether it was assisting my family with small tasks or learning new skills on my own.

As I grew older, that curiosity turned into action. I began teaching myself web development, sales, marketing, and communication through books, online resources, and hands-on projects. Every project became an opportunity to improve—not just technically, but also in discipline, problem-solving, and understanding how businesses create value.

Moving to Japan challenged me to adapt to a new environment, culture, and language. Rather than seeing those challenges as obstacles, I viewed them as opportunities to grow. Learning to communicate across cultures strengthened my resilience, independence, and willingness to step outside my comfort zone.

Today, my goal is to build a career in remote sales while continuing to expand my technical and entrepreneurial skills. I enjoy learning how businesses grow, how technology can solve real-world problems, and how meaningful relationships create long-term success.

I believe that experience is earned through consistent effort, curiosity, and a willingness to keep learning. Although I am still at the beginning of my professional journey, I am committed to improving every day and contributing wherever I can.
